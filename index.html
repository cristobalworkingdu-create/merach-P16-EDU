All in One Home Gym

Push. Pull. Squat. Row — all on one frame.

A Full Gym.38 sq ft.

Everything Has a Home

An integrated pegboard takes the handles and straps; plate horns take the weights. Everything you train with stays within arm’s reach of where you train.

The Posts Store, Too

3×3 in uprights front and rear share one hole pattern, so a storage mount bolts anywhere a training attachment does — including a vertical holder that stands the Olympic bar on the frame. Nothing leans against a wall between sessions.

Weight Off the Floor

Two built-in 145 lb stacks and 220 lb of plate capacity on the horns — all of it stored inside the footprint, none of it on your floor. One frame carries the resistance, the bar, the handles and the plates, in 38 sq ft.

Details You Feel Every Rep.

Machined aluminum pulleys and handles, knurled bars, braced footplate, one-handed trolley.

Machined Aluminum Throughout

Pulleys, handles, footplate are machined aluminum, not moulded plastic — one material standard across every contact point. Resistance stays even end to end, with no catch and no rattle.

Quiet, Guided Cable Routing

Dedicated routing guides open up the cable path while machined aluminum pulleys keep it smooth and quiet. A 2:1 ratio on independent left and right stacks lets you train one side at a time.

One-Handed Adjustment

The pulley trolley glides and locks with one hand, and the lat pulldown sets up the same way. Adjustments happen between sets, not instead of them.

Laser-Cut, Hand-Friendly Uprights

1 in holes on 2 in centers, laser-cut and deburred — reach through to set a J-hook and there’s no edge to catch your hand. Positions are numbered so you return to the same setting every session, and it’s the standard 1 in / 2 in pattern most rack attachments are built around.

Multi-Grip Pull-Up Bar

Knurled wide, narrow, overhand and neutral positions. The grip holds through sweat, so the set ends when the muscle does.

You Stay Seated

The leg holder pins your thighs across eleven positions, set to your leg length. Heavy pulldowns then pull your lats down instead of pulling you up off the seat.

Stack First, Plates on Top

A 145 lb weight stack per side changes load with one pin, so drop sets and quick swaps never stall the session. When the stack runs out, the extender peg takes another 220 lb of plates, for 365 lb per side without a single plate on the floor.
